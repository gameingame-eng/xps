# Code Of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We commit to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We expect everyone to act with professionalism and respect.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming, inclusive language.
- Focus feedback on code, design, and product decisions.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Keep discussions specific and factual.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or deliberate trolling.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional software project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this
code of conduct. Repeated or severe violations may result in a ban from future
participation.

If you need to report a problem, contact the maintainers through the project's
issue tracker or the preferred private channel for the repository.
